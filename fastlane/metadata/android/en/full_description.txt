Turn your Android smartphone into a real experimental physics laboratory with Physique Explicite Lab (Edited by Salimprof).

Designed for middle school physics and chemistry students and teachers (1AC, 2AC, 3AC) as well as science clubs, the app uses your phone's real hardware sensors to perform rigorous scientific measurements offline:

• Motion and acceleration: Accelerometer (X, Y, Z axes and magnitude in m/s²).
• Rotation and orientation: Gyroscope (angular velocity in rad/s) and inclination.
• Acoustics and sound: Microphone (audio oscilloscope, frequency measurement in Hz, FFT spectrum).
• Magnetism: Magnetometer (magnetic field in µT near a magnet or Earth's field).
• Light: Illuminance sensor (in lux).
• Atmospheric pressure: Barometer (in hPa).
• Location and movement: GPS (position, altitude and speed).

"LET'S EXPERIMENT" (J'EXPÉRIMENTE) MODULE & INTERACTIVE QUIZ:
Each guided activity leads students through 9 structured inquiry steps:
1. I discover — 2. My objective — 3. I prepare — 4. I perform (with direct launch of the real sensor measurement) — 5. I observe — 6. I describe — 7. I explain — 8. I conclude — 9. Check my understanding (interactive multiple-choice quiz with instant feedback and scientific explanations).

SCIENTIFIC FEATURES:
• Real-time scientific graphs with labelled axes and SI units.
• Local saving of measurement states and favourite experiments.
• Data export and sharing in CSV, TSV and Excel formats.
• Trilingual interface: French, Arabic (full RTL support) and English, with Light and Dark modes.
• Offline operation for local experiments, with no ads, no accounts, and no personal data collection.
